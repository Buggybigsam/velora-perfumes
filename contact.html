CONCIERGE FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about deliveries, payment, and authenticity in Ghana.

How does delivery and payment work?

We operate a high-touch Concierge Delivery model. You do not need to enter card details online. Once you place an order, our dispatch concierge verifies your location via WhatsApp or phone call. You only pay (via Cash or Mobile Money) upon receiving and inspecting your parcel.

How fast is shipping across Accra and other regions?

Orders within Greater Accra (East Legon, Cantonments, Airport, Tema, etc.) are delivered same-day or next-day. Deliveries to Kumasi, Takoradi, Cape Coast, and other regions take 24-48 hours via premium express parcel dispatch.

Are Velora fragrances 100% authentic?

Every Velora flacon is strictly genuine Extrait or Eau de Parfum, formulated in Grasse with botanical oils and packaged in weighted magnetic velvet boxes. We offer a 100% authenticity guarantee.

Can I request gift packaging or a personal message?

Yes! Simply leave a note in the "Optional Delivery Note" field during checkout or text our WhatsApp concierge (+233 24 555 0192) with your order reference (VEL-XXXXXX). We provide complimentary gold ribboning and custom handwritten note cards.

Need help with your order?

Our fragrance specialists are ready to assist you via WhatsApp.
